## Objectives 
A game where you are in a haunted house and you need to avoid the monsters! <BR>
Collect the candies to get points!  <BR>
You also get points by surviving longer.

## Controls
`Arrow keys` to move, and `WASD` to sidestep. Mobile controls are also supported.
