A simple first-person survival arcade-style game where you're trapped inside an infested mansion! Navigate the haunted house and dodge menacing monsters while collecting as much candy as you can before you get caught.

## Objective

Grab candies for points and rack up extra score the longer you stay alive.

## Controls

Up and Down arrow keys: Move forward and backward! <Br>
Left and Right arrow key: Turn the camera around! <br>
WASD: Sidestep! <br>
Mobile touch controls are also supported! <br>
