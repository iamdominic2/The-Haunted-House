A first-person arcade-style stealth game where you're trapped inside a mansion infested with scary monsters! Navigate the haunted house and collect as much candy as you can before you get killed!


## Objective

Grab candies for points and rack up extra score the longer you stay alive. <br>Orange candies give 5 points while green candies give 20 points!<br>Medkits recover your HP.<br>

## Enemies
Bats: Fast. <br>
Zombies: Powerful <br>
Ghosts: Phase through walls

## Controls

Up and Down arrow keys: Move forward and backward! <Br>
Left and Right arrow keys: Turn the camera around! <br>
WASD: Sidestep! <br>
Mobile touch controls are also supported! <br>
